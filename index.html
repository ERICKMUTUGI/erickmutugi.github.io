WHO WE ARE

More than a name. A way of thinking.

True SOUND Society™ (TSS) is a purpose-driven digital community and initiative promoting conscious consumption and responsible production.

We connect everyday choices with the outcomes they create — in agriculture, information, media, technology, business, culture, healthy living and community life.

TSS encourages people to question what they consume, understand its influence, and become intentional about what they produce, share and support.

The idea behind TSS grew from a simple conviction: the things people consume — food, information, media, ideas, technology and culture — influence what they eventually produce and contribute to society.

Through TSS, the founders seek to build a community that encourages conscious consumption, purposeful production and practical solutions across different areas of life.

OUR VISION

A world of conscious consumers and purposeful producers.

We envision communities where people understand the relationship between what they consume and what they produce — and use that understanding to create better outcomes.

OUR MISSION
